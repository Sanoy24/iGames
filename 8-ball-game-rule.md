# 1. Objective

The goal is to legally pocket all seven balls from your assigned group:

- Solids (1–7)
- Stripes (9–15)

After clearing your group, legally pocket the **8-ball** into a called pocket to win.

---

# 2. Equipment

## Balls

There are 16 balls:

- Cue Ball (white)
- 7 Solids (1-7)
- 7 Stripes (9-15)
- 8 Ball

## Table

Standard pool table:

- 6 pockets
- 4 corner pockets
- 2 side pockets

---

# 3. Ball Layout

The balls are racked in a triangle.

Rules:

- 8-ball in the center.
- One corner must be a solid.
- Other corner must be a stripe.
- Remaining balls random.

Example:

```
        1
      10  4
    12  8  5
   14 2 15 7
  3 11 6 13 9
```

---

# 4. Breaking

The first shot is called the break.

Requirements:

- Cue ball starts behind the head string.
- Strike the head ball first.

A legal break requires:

Option A

Pocket at least one ball.

OR

Option B

At least four object balls touch a rail.

If neither happens:

- Illegal break.
- Incoming player may:
    - accept table
    - re-rack
    - make opponent break again

---

# Break Scratch

If cue ball is pocketed:

- Break is legal if other conditions met (a pot or four balls to a rail).
- Incoming player receives ball in hand the region depends on the ruleset:
    - **BCA (Rule 7):** ball in hand **behind the head string** only.
    - **WPA / most app rulesets:** ball in hand **anywhere on the table**.

> Implementation note: iGames follows the WPA variant a break scratch gives
> ball in hand anywhere on the table.

---

# 5. Open Table

Immediately after the break, the table is usually open.

Open table means:

Neither player owns solids or stripes yet.

Example:

Player pockets:

- 3
- 12

Table is STILL OPEN.

Groups are not assigned until a legal called shot after the break.

---

# 6. Choosing Groups

Groups are assigned when:

- Table is open.
- Player legally pockets a called ball.

Example

Player calls:

"11 in side."

Makes it legally.

Player now owns:

Stripes

Opponent gets:

Solids

---

# 7. Legal Shot Requirements

Every shot must satisfy BOTH conditions.

Condition 1

Cue ball must first contact:

- one of your balls

(or any ball if table open)

Condition 2

After contact:

Either

- a ball is pocketed

OR

- any ball touches a rail

If neither occurs:

Foul.

---

# 8. Pocketing Balls

If you pocket one of your balls legally:

- Continue shooting.

Miss:

Turn ends.

Pocket opponent's ball:

Usually stays pocketed.

Turn depends on whether your called shot succeeded.

Pocket both:

Your own ball determines continuation.

---

# 9. Calling Shots

Most professional games require called shots.

You must call:

- object ball
- intended pocket

Example

Correct:

"5 ball, bottom right."

Not required:

- rails
- kisses
- banks
- caroms

As long as the called ball enters the called pocket.

---

# Slop

If the called ball enters a different pocket:

Turn ends.

Ball remains pocketed.

---

# 10. Fouls

Common fouls:

- Scratch
- Wrong ball first
- No rail after contact
- No ball contacted
- Cue ball leaves table
- Double hit
- Push shot
- Illegal jump
- Touching balls
- Moving balls by hand
- Shooting while balls moving

Result:

Opponent gets ball in hand.

---

# 11. Cue Ball Fouls

Includes:

Scratch

```
Cue ball enters pocket.
```

Off table

```
Cue ball jumps off table.
```

Wrong first contact

Example:

Player owns solids.

Hits stripe first.

Foul.

---

# 12. Scratch Rules

Scratch means:

Cue ball is pocketed.

Result:

Opponent receives ball in hand.

---

# 13. Ball in Hand

Player may place cue ball anywhere on table.

Can adjust position freely.

Cannot shoot until cue ball released.

---

# 14. Jump Shots

Legal only if:

Cue tip strikes downward cleanly.

Illegal:

Scooping under cue ball.

---

# 15. Push Shots

Illegal.

Cue tip cannot continue pushing cue ball after impact.

Cue must strike cleanly.

---

# 16. Frozen Balls

A ball touching a rail is frozen.

If your object ball is frozen:

After contact:

Need one of:

- another ball reaches rail
- cue ball reaches rail
- object ball leaves rail then returns

Otherwise:

Foul.

---

# 17. Combination Shots

Legal if:

First ball contacted belongs to shooter.

Example

Shooter owns solids.

Cue ball:

2 → 10 → pocket

Legal.

---

Illegal:

Cue ball:

10 → 2

Wrong first contact.

Foul.

---

# 18. Rail Requirements

After legal first contact:

Need:

- pocket a ball

OR

- any ball contacts rail

No rail:

Foul.

---

# 19. Winning the Game

To win:

1. Pocket all seven group balls.
2. Call pocket.
3. Pocket 8-ball legally.

Example

Player has only 8-ball left.

Calls:

"8 corner."

Makes it.

Wins.

---

# 20. Losing the Game

Immediate loss if:

Pocket 8-ball before clearing group.

Pocket the 8-ball on the **same stroke** as the last ball of your group (the 8 must be pocketed on a **separate, later** stroke BCA/WPA §20).

Pocket 8-ball in wrong pocket.

Scratch while pocketing 8-ball.

8-ball leaves table.

Pocket 8-ball on illegal shot.

Pocket 8-ball before groups assigned.

---

# 21. Common Examples

## Example 1

Player owns solids.

Hits:

3

Then:

3 enters pocket.

Legal.

Continue shooting.

---

## Example 2

Player owns solids.

Hits:

Stripe first.

Foul.

Opponent gets ball in hand.

---

## Example 3

Player owns stripes.

Hits:

11

No ball pockets.

Nothing reaches rail.

Foul.

---

## Example 4

Player owns solids.

Calls:

5 corner.

5 goes side pocket.

Miss.

Ball stays down.

Turn ends.

---

## Example 5

Player owns stripes.

Calls:

12 corner.

12 enters corner after several kisses.

Legal.

Continue.

---

## Example 6

Player legally pockets final stripe.

Calls:

8 side.

Makes 8.

Wins.

---

## Example 7

Player still has one stripe left.

Accidentally pockets 8.

Immediate loss.

---

## Example 8

Player pockets 8.

Cue ball scratches.

Immediate loss.

# 22. Strategy Basics

## Break

- Hit with power.
- Aim squarely at head ball.
- Try to spread balls evenly.

## Position Play

Always think one or two shots ahead.

Leave the cue ball for the next shot.

## Defense (Safety)

If no good shot exists:

Play a safety by leaving the opponent difficult position.

## Avoid Clusters

Separate grouped balls early.

## Save Key Balls

Don't pocket easy "insurance" balls too early.

Keep one available to recover position.

## Control the Cue Ball

Most games are won by cue ball control rather than shot difficulty.

---

# Summary

A legal shot requires:

- Correct first ball contacted.
- A ball pocketed OR a ball contacts a rail afterward.

A player continues shooting after legally pocketing one of their own balls.

Groups (solids/stripes) are assigned after the first legally pocketed called shot following the break.

The game is won by legally pocketing all group balls and then the 8-ball in a called pocket.

Common fouls result in the opponent receiving **ball in hand**.

Pocketing the 8-ball early, on the same stroke as your last group ball, in the wrong pocket, or scratching while pocketing it results in an immediate loss.
