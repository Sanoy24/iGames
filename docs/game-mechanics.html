How Players Win & Lose

Complete breakdown of the game structures, winning conditions, and payout mechanics for Keno and 90-Ball Bingo.

🎰 Keno

How a Keno Round Works

  1. Player picks numbers — e.g., picks 5 numbers: [3, 15, 27, 44, 68]
  2. Wallet is debited — ticket price is deducted immediately
  3. Draw happens — the system randomly draws 20 numbers from 1–80, e.g.:
    [2, 7, 15, 18, 22, 27, 33, 38, 41, 44, 49, 53, 58, 61, 65, 68, 72, 76, 80, 84]
  4. Matches are counted — player's [3, 15, 27, 44, 68] vs drawn numbers → 4 matches (15, 27, 44, 68)
  5. Paytable lookup — spots=5, matches=4 → multiplier = 25×
  6. Payout — 100 (stake) × 25 = 2,500 credits → wallet credited

When You WIN

✅
You win when your number of matches has a corresponding entry in the paytable. The payout = stake × multiplier.

When You LOSE

❌
You lose when your match count has NO entry in the paytable for your spot count. Payout = 0. Example: picked 5 spots but only matched 2 → no paytable entry → loss.

Three Prize Tiers

1️⃣

One Line

All 5 numbers in any single row have been drawn. First tier to settle.

2️⃣

Two Lines

All 5 numbers in any two rows have been drawn. Settles after one-line.

🏠

Full House

All 15 numbers on the ticket drawn. Ends the game.

How a Bingo Round Works

  1. Admin creates a room with ticket price, max tickets, and prizes per tier.
  2. Players buy tickets — 1 to 24 auto-generated tickets. Wallet debited per ticket.
  3. Numbers drawn one at a time — each draw triggers evaluation of ALL tickets.
  4. Tier settlement — when the first ticket(s) achieve a tier, that tier is settled immediately:
    • Prize is split evenly among all tickets that achieved the tier on that same draw.
    • Each tier is settled exactly once per room.
  5. Game ends when full house is achieved — all remaining active tickets marked as lost.

When You WIN

✅
You win when all 5 numbers in one or more rows of your ticket are drawn. You can win multiple tiers on the same ticket (one-line → two-lines → full house), accumulating payouts.

When You LOSE

❌
You lose when another player achieves full house before you. The game ends and your ticket is marked lost with payout = 0. However, you might still have won one-line or two-lines prizes earlier in the game.

Prize Splitting

If multiple tickets achieve a tier on the same drawn number, the prize is split:

Prize: 15,000 credits | Winners: 3 tickets
→ 15,000 ÷ 3 = 5,000 each

Prize: 10,000 credits | Winners: 3 tickets  
→ 10,000 ÷ 3 = 3,334 + 3,333 + 3,333
   (remainder goes to earlier ticket purchases)

The split is integer-safe — no floating point. Remainder credits go to the first winners (by ticket creation order).
